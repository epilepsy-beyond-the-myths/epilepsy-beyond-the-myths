Step 3 of 4 · What you can do

Seizure first aid: Stay. Safe. Side.

Stay

Stay calm and stay with the person until they are fully awake and alert. Time the seizure.

Safe

Move hard or sharp objects away and cushion the head. Never hold the person down.

Side

If they are not awake and aware, turn them gently onto their side so they can breathe.

Never put anything in their mouth.

Seizure lasting more than 5 minutes? Call emergency services: 112 in Georgia, 911 in the USA.

Source: Epilepsy Foundation, Seizure First Aid.
